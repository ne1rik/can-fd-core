Methodology

  • Vector corpus: frames extracted from CTU CAN FD reference captures, compared bit-by-bit against the model and RTL output. ACK slot excluded (driven by receiver).
  • Sweep: all four formats, DLC 0–15, BRS on/off, payload patterns 0x00 / 0xFF / alternating, RTL vs model.
  • Mutation: protocol-level faults injected into the reference model (CRC polynomial, CRC init, stuffing interval, stuff-count parity, reserved bits). A mutant must be detected.
  • Real timing: NBT/DBT with configurable TSEG1/TSEG2 and prescaler; phase switch armed at the BRS sample point and committed on the bit boundary.
